# JT9D High-Fidelity Steady-State Turbofan Simulator

## Introduction

This repository contains a professional-grade, modular 0D/1D steady-state thermodynamic performance simulator and digital twin for the **Pratt & Whitney JT9D** dual-spool turbofan engine. Built from the ground up in Python, the framework is architected around strict aeronautical engineering standards (such as EASA CS-E certification guidelines and DO-178C software structural best practices). 

The framework ingests raw NASA T-MATS empirical component maps, performs rigorous analytical design-point (DP) calibration against certified test cell telemetry, executes off-design operating lines via homotopy continuation, and performs multi-dimensional flight envelope sensitivity sweeps.

---

## Theoretical Framework, Mathematical Models & Assumptions

### 1. Thermodynamic Modeling & Gas Properties
* **Semi-Perfect Gas Formulation:** The working fluid transitions from clean ambient air in the cold section ($f = 0$) to combustion products containing fuel-air ratios ($f = \dot{m}_f / \dot{m}_{31}$) in the core. Specific heat ($C_p$), enthalpy ($h$), entropy ($s$), and ratio of specific heats ($\gamma$) are evaluated dynamically using high-accuracy NASA polynomial fits as a function of temperature and fuel-air ratio.
* **Mass & Energy Conservation:** Every component enforces strict continuity equations ($\sum \dot{m}_{\text{in}} = \sum \dot{m}_{\text{out}}$) and energy conservation balances across control volumes, accounting for cooling bleed extractions and enthalpy-weighted mixing processes.

### 2. Turbomachinery Aerodynamic Similarity
* **Corrected Parameters:** Component maps operate on non-dimensional similarity parameters, scaling physical mass flows and rotational speeds using local stagnation temperature ($\theta = T_t / 288.15$) and pressure ($\delta = P_t / 101325.0$) ratios referenced to ISA sea-level standard conditions.
* **Map Scaling Factors:** To reconcile raw NASA T-MATS maps with target engine telemetry, algebraic scaling factors ($SF_W, SF_{PR}, SF_{\eta}$) are bounded within strict physical limits ([0.85, 1.15]) during the design point optimization to prevent artificial geometric distortion.

### 3. Numerical Solvers & Convergence Architecture
* **Implicit Non-Linear System:** Off-design performance is governed by an 8-dimensional implicit vector of residuals (mass flow continuity across components and mechanical spool power equilibria for HP and LP shafts).
* **Robust Jacobian Engine:** Because choked components (such turbines and nozzles) yield rank-deficient Jacobians, the solver utilizes a **Newton-Raphson scheme coupled with the Moore-Penrose pseudo-inverse (`np.linalg.lstsq`)**. This guarantees stable convergence, avoids algebraic collapse, and projects descent directions onto active column spaces even near sonic boundaries.

### 4. Key Assumptions
* **0D/1D Quasi-Steady Operation:** Spatial variations across cross-sections are lumped into average stagnation properties at defined station boundaries (Stations 2, 21, 25, 3, 31, 4, 45, 5, 13, 18).
* **Spool Mechanical Equilibrium:** Power generated by the High-Pressure Turbine (HPT) matches the power demanded by the High-Pressure Compressor (HPC) plus accessory losses, while the Low-Pressure Turbine (LPT) drives the Fan and Low-Pressure Compressor (LPC).
